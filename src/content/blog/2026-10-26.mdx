It's been a while since I put my website online. I kind of left it aside to focus on a new project: HelpDesk.

I designed it mainly to practise a tech stack that seems to show up a lot in the job listings I've been looking at lately.

It's really hard to find a job in the field I studied. It makes no sense... Just yesterday, I found a """JUNIOR""" listing on InfoJobs asking for at least 3 years of experience?

That makes no sense (⇀‸↼‶). The most accessible option I have is taking a course that includes a work placement. I could also look at websites that offer remote jobs. I'm not really sure how those work, but I'll try to check them out in the future.

Enough about jobs. Today I made a few small changes to my website. I updated the welcome message to make it feel more "personal" and less corporate. I did that based on the 3 bits of feedback I got from people I showed the site to.

There are still things to improve, but for now it's not heading in a bad direction. Either way, I decided to expand the idea behind these blog posts a little and add images, videos, GIFs, and so on...
